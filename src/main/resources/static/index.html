ABOUT LIT

More Than a WhatsApp
Group. A Community.

London Indian Tamils (LIT) is a growing community bringing together Tamil-speaking people of Indian origin living across the UK — a place to meet people, make friends, discover opportunities, support one another and celebrate our culture.

A place to build friendships, share opportunities, support businesses and careers, celebrate our culture and participate in social, educational, sporting and community activities.
